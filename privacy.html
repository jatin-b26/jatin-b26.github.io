Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 September 2026

This policy covers the Reminder Bot app and the Google Sheet account it uses. It is written plainly: what is handled, how it is used, and how to reach the owner.

1. What we handle

  • The WhatsApp message you send to the bot, including its text and the time you choose for the reminder.
  • Your WhatsApp phone number, used only so the reminder can be sent back to you.
  • Timestamps of when a reminder is created and when it is delivered.

2. How it is used

The data exists for one purpose: to deliver the reminder you asked for, on time. Nothing else is built on top of it. There is no advertising, no user profiles, and no analytics.

3. Where it is stored

  • Google Sheets holds your reminders (text, time, phone number) in a private spreadsheet owned by the operator.
  • WhatsApp / Meta carries messages between you and the bot over the WhatsApp Business Cloud API.
  • A private server runs the scheduler that checks the spreadsheet every minute.

4. Sharing

Reminder data is not sold, rented, or given to anyone. The only parties with any access are the ones named in section 3, strictly to operate the service.

5. Retention and deletion

Rows stay in the spreadsheet until a reminder has been delivered and you remove them, or until you contact the owner to delete them. Ask and it is done.

6. Security

  • Access to the spreadsheet and the bot is private and authenticated; no public sign-up exists.
  • Credentials are scoped to the minimum required files and are stored in an encrypted vault.

7. Your choices

You can stop using the service at any time by sending a message such as stop, and you can ask for your stored reminders to be deleted.

8. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: notjatin.b26@gmail.com
