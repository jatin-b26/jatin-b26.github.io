How the bot handles data

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 September 2026

Personal bot, not a product. No ads. No analytics. No tracking. The bot keeps the message and time only to send your reminder back.

1. What the bot stores

  • The WhatsApp message you send, so the AI parse step can turn it into task + time.
  • Your WhatsApp number, only as the destination for the ping.
  • Timestamps for when a reminder was created and when the cron marked it sent.

2. Why

One job: deliver the ping you asked for, on time. Nothing is used for profiles, ads, or analytics.

3. Where it lives

  • Google Sheets holds the rows (task, time, number) in a private spreadsheet.
  • WhatsApp / Meta carries the chat over the WhatsApp Business Cloud API.
  • A private server runs the cron that checks the sheet every minute.

4. Sharing

Rows are not sold, rented, or handed out. Only the pieces in section 3 touch the data, and only to run the bot.

5. Keep and clear

Rows stay until the reminder is delivered and you clear them, or until you ask for deletion by email. Email and the row goes.

6. Access

  • Sheet and bot access are private. No public sign-up exists.
  • Credentials are scoped to the minimum files and stored encrypted.

7. Your side

This is a personal bot. Message it or stop messaging it whenever you want. Email to delete anything stored.
