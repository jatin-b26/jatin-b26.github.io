How it actually behaves

What the workflow does in the thread: how free language gets parsed, exact reply strings when it works or fails, how the minute cron sends the ping, and what the AI step does not do.

How do I message it?

Free language. Say the task and the time, for example call the dentist tomorrow at 9am. An AI step on the workflow parses it into task + time. No fixed template required when the model can parse it.

What if it does not understand?

It replies: I didn't understand. Please say: "Remind me to [task] at [time]". Use that hint on the next try. Delivery and read receipts are ignored.

What does a successful save look like?

Got it! I'll remind you: "…" at dd/MM/yyyy, HH:mm after the row is written to the sheet.

How does the ping look?

When the cron finds a due unsent row it sends ⏰ Reminder: [task] to the same number and marks the row sent so it does not fire twice.

Is the AI a full chat agent?

No. The model only parses reminder wording into task + time, or fails into the hint.

Any ads or analytics?

No third-party scripts on this page. Reminders are not used for profiles.
